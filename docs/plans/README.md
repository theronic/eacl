# Implementation plans (historical)

Planning notes written while individual changes were being designed, from
the v6/v7 Datalog-rule era through the v8 candidates. They record the
reasoning of their time and are not maintained as current guidance: the
mechanisms many of them describe (Datalog rules, `impl_fixed`, cursor trees,
per-path frontiers, the acyclic/merge routes) were replaced by the
stable-discovery engine on 2026-08-14. Current contracts live in
[`../stable-discovery-engine.md`](../stable-discovery-engine.md), the
OpenSpec main specs under `openspec/specs/`, and the dated reports under
[`../reports/`](../reports/).
